# Эпизод 1 — The Island Printed Too Many Coconuts (Inflation Explained)

Длина ~5–6 мин (для рекламы в середине нужно 8+: добавить вторую реальную историю и сцену с Шелли). Рассказчик (NARRATOR, голос George); герои — короткие реплики своими голосами.
В квадратных скобках — что на экране (для автора: какие рисунки нужны; для Claude: монтаж).

---

**[Пляж, общий вид острова. Тишина, волны.]**
NARRATOR: One morning on Coconut Republic, a coconut cost one coconut.
**[Ценник «1» → «100», удар.]**
NARRATOR: By lunch, it cost a hundred.
**[Тики в шоке держит один кокос.]**
TIKI: …I just wanted breakfast.
**[Заставка: COCONUT REPUBLIC · Episode 1: Inflation]**

**[Роща. Тики лезет на пальму.]**
NARRATOR: To understand how that happened, meet Tiki. Tiki climbs palm trees. Every day, he picks ten coconuts.
NARRATOR: But Tiki doesn't eat all ten. He brings them to the Pelican.
**[Дворец. Большой Билл ставит красную печать на кокос.]**
NARRATOR: This is Big Bill, the leader of the island. Big Bill does one very important thing. He stamps coconuts.
BIG BILL: Stamp! Now it's money.
NARRATOR: A plain coconut is food. A coconut with Bill's stamp is money. You can't eat it. Well, you can. But you'd be eating your savings.

**[Рынок. Крабби за прилавком. Ценники.]**
NARRATOR: On the island, there are exactly one hundred stamped coconuts. And there are exactly one hundred fish at Crabby's market.
NARRATOR: So one fish costs… one coconut. Simple.
CRABBY: Fish! One coco! Very fresh! Mostly.
NARRATOR: This is the most important idea in this whole video. Prices are just money, divided by stuff. One hundred coconuts. One hundred fish. One coconut per fish.

**[Дворец. Билл думает.]**
NARRATOR: Now, Big Bill has a problem. He wants to build a giant statue. Of himself. And he doesn't have enough coconuts.
BIG BILL: Taxes are unpopular. Borrowing is boring.
**[Билл смотрит на печать. Свет падает на неё.]**
BIG BILL: …But I have a stamp.
NARRATOR: So Bill stamps a hundred more coconuts. Just like that. He pays the builders, the builders go shopping, and everyone is happy.
**[Пип с микрофоном.]**
PIP: Breaking news! Everybody is rich! Everybody is rich!

**[Рынок. Очередь у лавки Крабби.]**
NARRATOR: For about one afternoon.
NARRATOR: Because here is the thing. There are now two hundred coconuts on the island. But there are still only one hundred fish.
**[Крабби смотрит на очередь, меняет ценник 1 → 2.]**
NARRATOR: Crabby sees a long line, and a lot of coconuts. So Crabby does what any crab would do.
CRABBY: Two coco. Same fish. Special price.
NARRATOR: Twice the money. Same amount of stuff. Prices double. That, right there, is inflation.

**[Шелли у доски. Мел: «MORE MONEY ≠ MORE STUFF».]**
SHELLY: Printing money doesn't make more fish. It just makes every coconut worth less fish.

**[Тики дома, грустный, считает 10 кокосов.]**
NARRATOR: Now look at who wins, and who loses.
NARRATOR: Tiki still earns ten coconuts a day. Nobody raised his pay. But everything now costs twice as much. Tiki just got poorer, without anyone taking a single coconut from him.
NARRATOR: Grandma Turtle saved coconuts for fifty years. Her savings just lost half their value. Overnight.
**[Билл у статуи, довольный.]**
NARRATOR: And Big Bill? Bill spent the new coconuts first. Before prices went up. The first person to spend new money always wins.
**[Доска: «NEW MONEY: FIRST SPENDER WINS, LAST ONE PAYS».]**

**[Дворец. Статуя без головы. Билл в панике.]**
NARRATOR: But the statue isn't finished. The builders want more coconuts, because everything costs more now.
BIG BILL: Fine. Stamp! Stamp! Stamp!
**[Монтаж ускоряется: печать — ценник 2 → 4 → 8 → 16 → 100, звук ускоряется.]**
NARRATOR: More coconuts. Higher prices. More coconuts. Higher prices.
NARRATOR: Economists have a name for when this gets out of control. Hyperinflation.
**[Тики везёт тачку кокосов за одной рыбой.]**
TIKI: One fish, please.
CRABBY: That'll be the whole wheelbarrow.

**[Переход: «MEANWHILE, IN THE REAL WORLD…». Карта, старые фото (рисуем, не копируем фото).]**
NARRATOR: This sounds like a cartoon. It isn't.
NARRATOR: Germany, 1923. The government printed money to pay its bills. By November, a single loaf of bread cost around two hundred billion marks. People were paid twice a day, and ran to the shops before prices changed again.
**[Рисунок: тачка с купюрами; ценник с длинным числом.]**
NARRATOR: Zimbabwe, 2008. Prices were doubling roughly every day. The country printed a one hundred trillion dollar note. It was not enough to buy a bus ticket for very long.
**[Рисунок: купюра 100 000 000 000 000.]**
NARRATOR: Same story both times. More and more money. The same amount of stuff.

**[Шелли у доски, спокойно.]**
NARRATOR: So is all inflation bad? Not exactly.
SHELLY: A little is normal. Many central banks aim for about two percent a year. Slow, and predictable.
NARRATOR: The problem isn't prices going up a little. The problem is prices going up fast, and nobody knowing how fast.

**[Доска: три урока, появляются по одному.]**
NARRATOR: Three things to remember.
NARRATOR: One. Prices are money divided by stuff. More money, same stuff, higher prices.
NARRATOR: Two. Inflation is a hidden tax on people who save, and on people whose pay doesn't keep up.
NARRATOR: Three. Whoever gets the new money first, wins.

**[Вечер. Остров. Вулкан вдалеке тихо дымит.]**
NARRATOR: Big Bill finally finished his statue.
BIG BILL: Magnificent.
NARRATOR: But Tiki still can't afford breakfast. So tomorrow, he's going to do something brave.
**[Тики стоит у двери Банка Шелли.]**
NARRATOR: He's going to borrow coconuts.
NARRATOR: Next time, on Coconut Republic.
**[Заставка + «Subscribe to see what Shelly charges.»]**

## Факты (для описания)
- Германия, ноябрь 1923: буханка хлеба стоила порядка 200 млрд марок.
- Зимбабве, 2008: цены удваивались примерно каждые 24 часа (Hanke & Kwok, Cato Journal, 2009); банкнота 100 трлн Z$.
- Ориентир многих центробанков по инфляции — около 2% в год.
- *Not financial advice. For education and fun.*

## Shorts из эпизода
1. «Prices are money divided by stuff» — рынок, 1 рыба на 1 кокос → 2 кокоса (30 с).
2. «Who wins from printing money?» — Билл тратит первым (35 с).
3. «The wheelbarrow» — тачка за одну рыбу + Германия 1923 (40 с).
